Meet the Cast

The stars of Instagram's favorite fake couple.

Drea Santos
Age 28 · The Face
@dreadoesit — 800K followers. Built a brand on "authentic chaos." Beneath the curated mess is a former art student who remembers what it felt like to make something without an audience.
Benji Park
Age 30 · The Heart
@gentlebenji — 1.2M followers. The internet's "softboi" sweetheart. Former social worker who genuinely wanted to help people before he discovered helping people pays less than pretending to.
Cass Mendez
Age 45 · The Agent
Veteran agent of three influencer divorces and one NFT scandal. Exists primarily through FaceTime in increasingly expensive blazers. Not evil, just exhaustively pragmatic.
